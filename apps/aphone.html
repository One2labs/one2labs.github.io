Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to M Dialer (Phone), developed by One2 Labs.

Information Collection and Use

M Dialer is designed with a privacy-first philosophy.

M Dialer does not collect, store, or share your personal contacts, phone numbers, or call history with us for advertising purposes.

To provide core calling and contact management features, the app may request access to:

  • Contacts
  • Phone
  • Call Logs
  • Microphone (when required for calling features)

These permissions are used to provide app functionality on your device. Your contacts, phone numbers, and call history are not transmitted to our servers by the app for analytics or advertising purposes.

Firebase Analytics: M Dialer uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app and to improve its performance and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as app usage events, device information, and app-related identifiers. The information collected depends on the analytics configuration and the features used.

Firebase Analytics does not require access to your contacts, phone numbers, or call history. Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with its privacy policies.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy .

Some features allow copying phone numbers to your clipboard. Clipboard contents are managed by your operating system and may be accessible to other applications according to your device's security settings.

Log Data

M Dialer does not independently collect or store personal log data on its own servers. However, Firebase Analytics may collect app usage and technical information as configured in the app.

  • Firebase Analytics is used for usage analytics
  • App interaction events may be collected
  • Technical device and app information may be collected
  • Personal contacts and call history are not collected for analytics purposes

Advertising and Google AdMob

M Dialer uses Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your IP address, device information, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and information about ad impressions and interactions.

The information processed may vary depending on your device, region, consent choices, and advertising configuration.

M Dialer does not provide your contacts, phone numbers, or call history to Google AdMob for advertising.

Depending on your region, consent choices, and available advertising settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services .

Cookies & Tracking

M Dialer does not use cookies for its core app functionality.

The app uses Firebase Analytics, which may use app-related identifiers and similar technologies to measure app usage and improve the user experience.

Google AdMob may also use advertising identifiers and similar technologies to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

The availability and use of personalized advertising may depend on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the app's advertising configuration.

Third-Party Services

M Dialer uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies. These services include:

  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

Security

Your trust is important to us.

M Dialer is designed to keep your contacts, phone numbers, and call history on your device. These data are not provided to Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics by M Dialer for advertising or analytics purposes.

Information required by integrated third-party services may be transmitted to those service providers according to their respective configurations and privacy policies.

We are committed to keeping the app private, secure, and free from unnecessary data collection.
