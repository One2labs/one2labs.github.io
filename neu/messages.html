General

Neu Messages ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Messages is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Your private message content (SMS/MMS), contacts, phone numbers, and message-related data are processed on your device. One2 Labs does not transmit your private message content, contact information, or extracted phone numbers to its own servers.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Device Phone Number – Local Display

Data Accessed: Your mobile phone number(s) associated with your device's SIM card(s), where available and permitted by Android.

Purpose: To display SIM information inside the App and allow convenient SIM selection when sending messages.

Processing: This information is processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not upload your phone number to its servers.

Permission: READ_PHONE_STATE may be required for certain multi-SIM functionality.

1.2. SMS and MMS Messages

Data Accessed: SMS and MMS messages stored on your device, including message text, sender and recipient phone numbers, timestamps, and other message-related information.

Purpose: To provide core messaging functionality, including displaying, reading, organizing, searching, sending, and managing messages.

Processing: Message information is processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not upload your private SMS or MMS content to its servers.

1.3. Automatic OTP Code Detection

Neu Messages may analyze incoming SMS messages locally to identify One-Time Password (OTP) or verification codes.

Purpose: To make it easier for you to identify and copy verification codes when using supported messaging features.

OTP detection is performed locally on your device. Message content and detected OTP codes are not transmitted to One2 Labs servers.

1.4. Phone Number Extraction

Neu Messages may identify phone numbers contained within SMS messages.

Purpose: To allow you to conveniently copy or interact with phone numbers found in messages.

This processing occurs locally on your device and extracted phone numbers are not transmitted to One2 Labs servers.

1.5. Contacts

Data Accessed: Contact names, phone numbers, and other contact information stored in your device's address book.

Purpose: To display contact names, identify message senders, and provide contact suggestions within the messaging interface.

Processing: Contact information is accessed and processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not upload your contacts to its servers.

Permission: READ_CONTACTS may be required for contact-related functionality.

1.6. Clipboard Features

Neu Messages may provide functionality that allows you to copy OTP codes, phone numbers, message text, or other information to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Messages does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

1.7. Internet and Mobile Network Access

Neu Messages may use your mobile network or internet connection for messaging functionality, including:

  • Sending and receiving MMS messages through your mobile carrier or network.
  • Other carrier-supported messaging operations.
  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing, where applicable.

The transmission of messages through your mobile carrier or network is necessary to deliver messages to their intended recipients. One2 Labs does not intercept or store your message content on its own servers.

2. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Personal information used by Neu Messages, including SMS/MMS content, contacts, phone numbers, and message-related data, is primarily processed locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

No Cloud Storage of Message Data

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your private SMS/MMS content, contacts, or message history.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

If the App offers paid features or in-app purchases, Neu Messages may use Google Play Billing to process purchases.

Payment transactions are handled by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, such as transaction information and purchase status, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Neu Messages currently does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services to collect information about your messaging activity.

4. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your messages, contacts, phone numbers, or other private messaging information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

Messages sent through your mobile carrier or network may necessarily be processed by the carrier and relevant telecommunications infrastructure to deliver those messages. This processing is outside the control of One2 Labs.

Information may also be processed by Android, Google Play, or other platform services when required for the operation of your device, the App, or services you choose to use. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Messages does not maintain copies of your private messages or contacts on One2 Labs servers.

Because message and contact information is processed locally, you can manage or delete it using Neu Messages, your Android device settings, or other applications that manage your device's messages and contacts.

Information stored by Android or other applications may not be deleted when Neu Messages is uninstalled.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Neu Messages is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .
