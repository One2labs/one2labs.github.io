General

Neu Calculator ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Calculator is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Neu Calculator is designed to perform calculations locally on your device. The App does not collect, transmit, or share your calculator input, calculation history, or calculation results with One2 Labs or external servers.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Calculator Input and Calculations

Data Accessed: Numbers, mathematical expressions, calculations, and other information that you enter into the App.

Purpose: To perform mathematical calculations and display results.

Processing: Calculator input and calculations are processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not receive or collect your calculator input.

1.2. Calculation History

Neu Calculator may store calculation history locally on your device if this functionality is provided.

Purpose: To allow you to review previous calculations and reuse calculation results.

Calculation history is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to One2 Labs servers.

1.3. Clipboard Features

Neu Calculator may allow you to copy calculation results, numbers, or mathematical expressions to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Calculator does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

2. Permissions

Neu Calculator is designed to operate without requiring access to sensitive device permissions for its core calculator functionality.

The App does not require access to your contacts, call logs, messages, microphone, camera, photos, videos, or location to perform calculations.

If Android permissions are introduced for an optional feature in a future version, the applicable permission and its purpose will be disclosed in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

3. Internet Access

Neu Calculator does not require internet access for its core calculator functionality.

Calculator input, calculation results, and locally stored calculation history are not transmitted over the internet by the App.

4. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Calculator input, calculation results, and calculation history, where applicable, are processed and stored locally on your device.

No Cloud Storage

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your calculator input, calculation history, or calculation results.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your calculator input, calculation history, or calculation results with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

If you explicitly choose to copy information to the clipboard or use an Android sharing feature, the selected information may be provided to another application or service that you choose.

The handling of information by another application or service is governed by that application's or service's own privacy practices.

6. Log Data and Analytics

Neu Calculator does not collect analytics, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, or personal log data.

The App does not use third-party analytics SDKs or tracking technologies to monitor your calculator activity.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Neu Calculator does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

The App does not create behavioral tracking profiles based on your calculator usage.

8. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

Neu Calculator uses Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases and paid features offered through Google Play.

Payment transactions are processed by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, including transaction information and purchase status, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Neu Calculator does not currently use advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, or other third-party analytics services to collect information about your calculator activity.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Calculator does not maintain copies of your calculator input, calculation history, or calculation results on One2 Labs servers.

Any calculation history stored locally by the App can be managed or deleted through the App where such functionality is provided.

Information stored locally on your device may remain available according to Android's application storage behavior until it is deleted or the App's data is cleared.

Purchase-related records may be retained by Google Play according to Google's applicable policies and legal requirements.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

Neu Calculator is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .
