General

Neu Calculator ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Calculator is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Neu Calculator is designed to perform calculations primarily on your device. Your calculations, entered numbers, expressions, and calculation history are processed locally and are not transmitted to One2 Labs servers.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Calculator Input and Calculations

Data Accessed: Numbers, mathematical expressions, calculations, and other information that you enter into the App.

Purpose: To perform mathematical calculations and provide calculator functionality.

Processing: Calculation inputs and results are processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not upload your calculations or entered data to its servers.

1.2. Calculation History

Neu Calculator may store your calculation history on your device so that you can review previous calculations.

Data: Previous calculations, mathematical expressions, and results generated while using the App.

This information is stored and processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to One2 Labs servers.

1.3. Clipboard Features

Neu Calculator may allow you to copy calculations, numbers, expressions, or results to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Calculator does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

1.4. Device Information

Neu Calculator may use basic device information required by Android to operate the App correctly.

The App does not intentionally collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or other device information for analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking purposes.

1.5. Internet Access

Neu Calculator may require internet access for certain functionality, including:

  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Accessing optional online resources or support-related functionality, where applicable.
  • Other functionality that may be introduced in future versions of the App.

One2 Labs does not use internet connectivity to upload your private calculations, entered numbers, or calculation history to its own servers.

2. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Calculator inputs, calculations, results, and calculation history are primarily processed locally on your device.

No Cloud Storage of Calculation Data

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your private calculations, entered numbers, mathematical expressions, or calculation history.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

Neu Calculator uses Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases and paid features offered through Google Play.

Payment transactions are processed by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, including transaction information, purchase status, and other information necessary to complete and manage purchases, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Play Billing is used for purchase processing and does not provide One2 Labs with access to your calculations, entered numbers, expressions, or calculation history.

Neu Calculator currently does not use advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, or third-party analytics services to collect information about your calculator activity.

4. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your calculations, entered numbers, mathematical expressions, or calculation history with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

When you explicitly choose to copy or share information using Android's clipboard or sharing functionality, the selected information may be provided to the application or service you choose.

Information may also be processed by Android, Google Play, or other platform services when required for the operation of your device, the App, or services you choose to use. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Calculator does not maintain copies of your private calculations or calculation history on One2 Labs servers.

Calculation history stored locally can be managed or deleted using the App or your device settings, depending on the functionality provided by the App.

Information stored by Android or other applications may not be deleted when Neu Calculator is uninstalled.

Purchase-related records may be retained by Google Play according to Google's applicable policies and legal requirements.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Neu Calculator is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .

7. Open Source Components

Neu Calculator may include open-source components or code from third-party open-source projects. Applicable open-source licenses and notices govern the use and distribution of those components.

Where applicable, the relevant open-source license information is provided within the App or its accompanying source code.

Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Policy.

We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
