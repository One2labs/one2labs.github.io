General

Neu Voice Recorder ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Voice Recorder is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Your audio recordings, voice notes, and recording-related information are processed primarily on your device. One2 Labs does not automatically upload your private recordings to its own servers. Audio files may be transmitted externally only when you explicitly initiate an action such as sharing or exporting a recording.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Microphone and Audio Recording

Data Accessed: Audio captured through your device's microphone when you explicitly start an audio recording.

Purpose: To provide voice and audio recording functionality.

Processing: Audio recording is processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not automatically upload your recordings to its servers.

Permission: RECORD_AUDIO is required to record audio.

1.2. Audio Files and Recording Metadata

Data Accessed: Audio recordings created through the App and related information such as file names, durations, dates, timestamps, and file metadata.

Purpose: To save, play, rename, organize, search, and manage recordings.

Processing: Recordings and related metadata are processed locally on your device.

1.3. Storage and Media Access

Depending on your Android version and the features you use, Neu Voice Recorder may use Android storage or media frameworks to save and manage recordings.

These mechanisms may allow the App to:

  • Save audio recordings.
  • Read recordings created or selected by you.
  • Rename, organize, or delete recordings.
  • Share recordings when you explicitly initiate sharing.

Storage and media access is used only to provide the corresponding recording functionality.

1.4. Clipboard Features

Neu Voice Recorder may provide functionality that allows you to copy recording names, file paths, or other text to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Voice Recorder does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

1.5. Sharing and Exporting Recordings

Neu Voice Recorder may allow you to share or export audio recordings using Android's standard sharing functionality.

Processing: Sharing or exporting occurs only when you explicitly initiate the action. The selected recording may then be provided to the application or service you choose.

Once a recording is shared with another application or service, its handling is governed by that application's or service's privacy practices.

1.6. Internet Access

Neu Voice Recorder may require internet access for certain functionality, including:

  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Accessing optional online resources or support-related functionality, where applicable.
  • Other functionality that may be introduced in future versions of the App.

One2 Labs does not use the internet connection to automatically upload your private audio recordings to its own servers.

2. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Personal information used by Neu Voice Recorder, including audio recordings and recording metadata, is primarily processed locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

No Cloud Storage of Recordings

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your private audio recordings or voice notes.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock and device encryption.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

If the App offers paid features or in-app purchases, Neu Voice Recorder may use Google Play Billing to process purchases.

Payment transactions are handled by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, such as transaction information and purchase status, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Neu Voice Recorder currently does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services to collect information about your recordings or recording activity.

4. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your audio recordings, voice notes, or recording-related information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

When you explicitly choose to share a recording using Android's sharing functionality, the selected recording may be provided to the application or service you choose. Such processing is controlled by that application or service.

Information may also be processed by Android, Google Play, or other platform services when required for the operation of your device, the App, or services you choose to use. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Voice Recorder does not maintain copies of your audio recordings or voice notes on One2 Labs servers.

Because your recordings are stored on your device, you can manage or delete them using Neu Voice Recorder, Android, or other applications that have access to your device's media storage.

Deleting a recording through Neu Voice Recorder may permanently remove the selected file from your device, depending on Android system behavior and the action you choose. We recommend confirming deletion actions carefully.

Information stored by Android or other applications may not be deleted when Neu Voice Recorder is uninstalled.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Neu Voice Recorder is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .
