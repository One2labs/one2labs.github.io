Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

One2 Labs ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Neu Phone – Smart Dialer mobile application (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use Neu Phone and describes our commitment to protecting your privacy.

Information Collection and Use

Neu Phone is designed with privacy in mind. The application primarily processes information locally on your device to provide its core phone and contact management features.

Neu Phone may access information such as:

  • Contacts stored on your device
  • Phone numbers
  • Call history and recent calls
  • Favorite contacts
  • Blocked numbers
  • Information required to place and manage phone calls

This information is used only to provide the functionality of the application, such as displaying and searching contacts, managing recent calls, making calls, managing favorites, and blocking unwanted calls.

Neu Phone does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Your contacts and call history are intended to remain on your device and are not uploaded to One2 Labs servers for advertising, profiling, or analytics purposes.

Neu Phone's core calling and contact-management functionality operates primarily on your device.

Permissions

Neu Phone may request Android permissions that are necessary to provide its functionality.

Depending on the features you use, these permissions may include:

  • Contacts: To display, search, and manage contacts.
  • Call logs: To display recent, missed, and other call history.
  • Phone/Call permissions: To place and manage phone calls.
  • Default Phone/Dialer role: To provide the full functionality of a phone dialer when you choose to set Neu Phone as your default phone application.
  • Notifications: Where required to provide relevant calling or post-call functionality.

These permissions are used only for the features they enable. You can manage permissions through your Android device settings.

Data Stored on Your Device

Information used by Neu Phone, including contacts and call history, is generally stored and processed locally on your Android device.

One2 Labs does not intentionally upload your contacts, call history, or call-related content to its own servers.

If you uninstall Neu Phone or clear its application data, information stored by the application may be removed according to Android's system behavior. Information that belongs to other applications or the Android operating system may remain unaffected.

Clipboard

Neu Phone may provide functionality that allows you to copy phone numbers or other text to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard may be accessible to the Android operating system and to other applications that have permission to access clipboard data.

Neu Phone does not control how other applications or the operating system process information stored in the clipboard.

Log Data

Neu Phone does not intentionally collect personal or technical log data for tracking, advertising, or profiling purposes.

Certain technical information may nevertheless be processed automatically by Android, Google Play, or other system services operating on your device. Such processing is controlled by those services and their respective privacy policies.

Cookies

The Neu Phone mobile application does not use cookies.

Cookies are generally associated with websites and web-based services rather than the core functionality of this Android application.

Third-Party Services

Neu Phone does not intentionally transmit your contacts, call history, or call-related content to third-party services.

The application is distributed through Google Play and operates on the Android platform. Google Play, Android, and other system services may independently process certain information in accordance with their respective terms and privacy policies.

Data Security

Your trust is important to us. Neu Phone is designed to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information.

Because most personal information used by the application is processed locally on your device, you retain control over that information through your Android device and its security settings.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information processed by your device, the Android operating system, Google Play, or other third-party services.

Children's Privacy

Neu Phone is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Neu Phone. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Neu Phone is designed to process contacts and call history locally, One2 Labs generally does not maintain copies of this information on its own servers.

You can remove application data through your Android device settings or uninstall the application.

If you have contacted One2 Labs directly by email or otherwise provided information to us outside the application, we may retain that information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

You may contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com regarding questions about personal information you have provided directly to us.
