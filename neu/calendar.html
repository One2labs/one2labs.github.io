General

Neu Calendar ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Calendar is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Your calendar events, reminders, schedules, and related calendar information are processed on your device. One2 Labs does not transmit your private calendar information to its own servers.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Calendar Events

Data Accessed: Calendar events, event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, recurrence information, reminders, and other calendar information stored on your device, depending on the information available in your calendars.

Purpose: To display, create, edit, delete, search, organize, and manage calendar events within the App.

Processing: Calendar information is accessed and processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not upload your calendar events to its servers.

1.2. Calendar Accounts

Neu Calendar may display calendar accounts and calendars that are already configured on your Android device.

Purpose: To allow you to view and manage events associated with calendars available through your device.

Neu Calendar does not collect or store your account passwords or authentication credentials.

Calendar synchronization with an account is handled by Android and the relevant calendar provider according to that provider's settings and privacy policy.

1.3. Notifications and Reminders

Neu Calendar may schedule notifications for calendar events and reminders.

Purpose: To remind you about upcoming events and scheduled activities.

Reminder notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. Neu Calendar does not transmit your event information to One2 Labs servers for notification purposes.

Permission: POST_NOTIFICATIONS may be required on supported Android versions.

1.4. Clipboard Features

Neu Calendar may provide functionality that allows you to copy event information, locations, notes, or other text to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Calendar does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

1.5. Internet Access

Neu Calendar may require internet access for certain functionality, including:

  • Synchronization performed by Android or your selected calendar provider.
  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Accessing optional online resources or support-related functionality, where applicable.
  • Other functionality that may be introduced in future versions of the App.

One2 Labs does not use the internet connection to upload your private calendar events or personal calendar information to its own servers.

2. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Personal information used by Neu Calendar, including calendar events, reminders, schedules, and related information, is primarily processed locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

No Cloud Storage by One2 Labs

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your private calendar events or personal calendar information.

If you use calendar synchronization provided by Android, Google, or another calendar provider, your calendar data may be synchronized according to that provider's settings and privacy policy. Such synchronization is separate from One2 Labs.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

If the App offers paid features or in-app purchases, Neu Calendar may use Google Play Billing to process purchases.

Payment transactions are handled by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, such as transaction information and purchase status, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Neu Calendar currently does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services to collect information about your calendar activity.

4. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your calendar events, reminders, schedules, or other private calendar information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

If your device is configured to synchronize calendars with Google or another calendar provider, that provider may process your calendar information according to your settings and its applicable privacy policy.

Information may also be processed by Android, Google Play, or other platform services when required for the operation of your device, the App, or services you choose to use. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Calendar does not maintain copies of your private calendar events or personal calendar information on One2 Labs servers.

Because calendar information is managed through your device, you can manage or delete events using Neu Calendar or other calendar applications connected to your device.

If a calendar is synchronized with an external account, deleting an event may also affect the synchronized calendar depending on the account and synchronization settings.

Information stored by Android or other applications may not be deleted when Neu Calendar is uninstalled.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Neu Calendar is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .
