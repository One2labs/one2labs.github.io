Privacy Policy (Neu Files)

Last updated: March 12, 2026

General

Neu Files ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Files is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Your files, folders, documents, images, videos, audio files, and storage-related information are processed primarily on your device. One2 Labs does not automatically upload your private file content to its own servers. Files are transmitted externally only when you explicitly initiate an action such as FTP transfer or file sharing.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Files and Folders

Data Accessed: Files and folders stored on your device, including documents, images, videos, audio files, downloads, archives, and other supported file types.

Purpose: To browse, display, search, organize, create, rename, move, copy, delete, and manage files and folders using the App's file-management functionality.

Processing: File and folder information is accessed and processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not automatically upload your files to its servers.

1.2. File Metadata

Data Accessed: File names, paths, sizes, dates, timestamps, file types, permissions, and other metadata available through the Android storage system.

Purpose: To display, search, sort, filter, organize, and manage files.

Processing: File metadata is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to One2 Labs servers.

1.3. Storage Information

Neu Files may access information about available and used storage space on your device.

Purpose: To display storage information and help you manage available device storage.

This information is processed locally on your device.

1.4. Storage and Media Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the features you use, Neu Files may request permissions or access through Android's storage frameworks to manage files and media.

These permissions or access mechanisms may allow the App to:

  • Browse and display files and folders.
  • Create, rename, move, copy, or delete files and folders.
  • Organize device storage.
  • Search and sort files.
  • Access selected files that you explicitly choose.
  • Share files when you explicitly initiate a sharing action.

These permissions are used only to provide the corresponding file-management functionality.

1.5. Clipboard Features

Neu Files may provide functionality that allows you to copy file names, file paths, text, or other information to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Files does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

1.6. FTP File Transfer

Neu Files may provide FTP functionality that allows you to connect to an FTP server and transfer files between your device and the server.

Data Accessed: Files and folders that you explicitly select for upload or download, along with the server address, port, username, and other connection information required to establish the FTP connection.

Purpose: To allow you to transfer files between your device and an FTP server of your choice.

Processing: FTP transfers are initiated by you. Files selected for transfer are transmitted between your device and the FTP server that you configure. One2 Labs does not operate or control those FTP servers and does not receive copies of files transferred through this feature.

Important: The privacy and security of files transferred through FTP depend on the FTP server, network connection, and server configuration you choose. Standard FTP does not encrypt data during transmission. Where available, we recommend using a secure alternative such as FTPS or SFTP.

1.7. Internet and Network Access

Neu Files may use an internet or local network connection for certain functionality, including:

  • FTP file transfers initiated by the user.
  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Accessing optional online resources or support-related functionality, where applicable.
  • Other network functionality that may be introduced in future versions of the App.

Neu Files does not automatically upload your files to One2 Labs servers. Files are transmitted externally only when you explicitly initiate a supported transfer, such as an FTP upload or file-sharing action.

2. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Personal information used by Neu Files, including file names, paths, metadata, and file content, is primarily processed locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

No Cloud Storage of File Data

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your private files, folders, documents, photos, videos, or other personal storage content.

FTP Connection Information

If you save FTP connection profiles within Neu Files, connection information such as server addresses, ports, usernames, or other configuration details may be stored locally on your device to allow you to reconnect to the configured server.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any FTP accounts or servers that you configure.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock and device encryption.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

If the App offers paid features or in-app purchases, Neu Files may use Google Play Billing to process purchases.

Payment transactions are handled by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, such as transaction information and purchase status, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Neu Files currently does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services to collect information about your files or storage activity.

4. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your files, folders, documents, photos, videos, or other private storage information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

When you explicitly choose to transfer files using the FTP functionality, the selected files are transmitted to the FTP server you configure. One2 Labs does not operate or control those servers and does not receive copies of files transferred through FTP.

When you explicitly choose to share a file using Android's sharing functionality, the selected file may be provided to the application or service you choose. Such processing is controlled by that application or service.

Information may also be processed by Android, Google Play, or other platform services when required for the operation of your device, the App, or services you choose to use. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Files does not maintain copies of your files, folders, or personal storage content on One2 Labs servers.

Because your files are stored on your device, you can manage or delete them using Neu Files, Android, or other applications that have access to your device's storage.

FTP connection profiles and related configuration information stored locally by the App can be removed through the App where such functionality is provided.

Deleting a file through Neu Files may permanently remove the selected file from your device, depending on Android system behavior and the action you choose. We recommend confirming deletion actions carefully.

Information stored by Android or other applications may not be deleted when Neu Files is uninstalled.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Neu Files is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .
