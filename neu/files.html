General

Neu Files ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Files is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Your files, folders, documents, images, videos, audio files, and storage-related information are processed on your device. One2 Labs does not automatically upload your private files or file contents to its own servers.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Files and Folders

Data Accessed: Files and folders stored on your device, including documents, images, videos, audio files, downloads, archives, and other supported file types.

Purpose: To browse, display, search, organize, create, rename, copy, move, delete, and manage files and folders.

Processing: File information and file contents are accessed and processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not automatically upload your files to its servers.

1.2. File Metadata and Storage Information

Data Accessed: File names, file paths, file sizes, timestamps, file types, folder information, storage capacity, and available device storage.

Purpose: To provide file browsing, sorting, searching, filtering, storage information, and file management functionality.

This information is processed locally on your device.

1.3. Storage and Media Access

Depending on your Android version and the features you use, Neu Files may request access to files and media through Android's storage frameworks.

This access may be used to:

  • Browse and display files.
  • Create, rename, move, copy, or delete files and folders.
  • Organize device storage.
  • Search and sort files.
  • Open files using compatible applications.
  • Allow user-initiated file sharing.

These permissions or access mechanisms are used only to provide the corresponding file management functionality.

1.4. File Sharing and Exporting

Neu Files may allow you to share or export files using Android's standard sharing functionality.

Sharing or exporting occurs only when you explicitly initiate the action. The selected files may then be provided to the application or service you choose.

Once a file is shared with another application or service, its handling is governed by that application's or service's privacy practices.

1.5. FTP Functionality

Neu Files may provide FTP functionality that allows you to transfer files between your Android device and an FTP server or compatible network service.

FTP transfers are initiated and controlled by you. When you connect to an FTP server and upload or download files, the selected files and associated connection information are transmitted directly between your device and the FTP server you specify.

One2 Labs does not operate or control FTP servers used by you and does not receive copies of files transferred through your chosen FTP server.

Important: The privacy and security of FTP transfers depend on the FTP server, network, credentials, and connection method you choose. FTP without encryption may transmit information in an unencrypted form.

You should use secure FTP protocols such as FTPS or SFTP when supported by your server and network environment.

1.6. Clipboard Features

Neu Files may allow you to copy file names, file paths, or selected text to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Files does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

1.7. Internet Access

Neu Files may use network connectivity for functionality that you explicitly initiate or require, including:

  • FTP or other network-based file transfers initiated by you.
  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Accessing optional online resources or support-related functionality, where applicable.
  • Other network functionality that may be introduced in future versions of the App.

One2 Labs does not use internet connectivity to automatically upload your private files or file contents to its own servers.

2. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Files, folders, file contents, metadata, and storage-related information are primarily processed locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

No Cloud Storage of Private Files

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your private files, documents, photos, videos, audio files, or other personal file content.

Files transferred by you to an FTP server are stored by that FTP server according to its configuration and the policies of its operator. One2 Labs does not control those servers.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock and device encryption.

You are responsible for protecting FTP credentials and connection information used with servers you configure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

Neu Files uses Google Play Billing to process in-app purchases and paid features offered through Google Play.

Payment transactions are processed by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, including transaction information, purchase status, and other information necessary to complete and manage purchases, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Google Play Billing is used for purchase processing and does not provide One2 Labs with access to your files, folders, documents, or private file contents.

Neu Files currently does not use advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics, or third-party analytics services to collect information about your file-management activity.

4. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your files, documents, photos, videos, audio files, or private file information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

When you explicitly choose to share a file using Android's sharing functionality, the selected file may be provided to the application or service you choose.

When you explicitly use FTP functionality, selected files are transmitted to or received from the FTP server you specify. One2 Labs does not control the handling of files by that server.

Information may also be processed by Android, Google Play, or other platform services when required for the operation of your device, the App, or services you choose to use. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Files does not maintain copies of your private files, documents, photos, videos, or other file contents on One2 Labs servers.

Files stored on your device can be managed or deleted using Neu Files, Android, or another application with appropriate access to your device's storage.

Files uploaded by you to an FTP server may remain on that server until you or the server operator deletes them.

Information stored by Android or other applications may not be deleted when Neu Files is uninstalled.

Purchase-related records may be retained by Google Play according to Google's applicable policies and legal requirements.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Neu Files is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .
