General

Neu Gallery ("the App") is developed by One2 Labs.

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how information accessed or processed by Neu Gallery is handled.

Core Privacy Principle: Your photos, videos, albums, folders, and related media information are processed on your device. One2 Labs does not transmit your private media content to its own servers.

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

1.1. Photos and Videos

Data Accessed: Photos, images, videos, and other media files stored on your device.

Purpose: To display, browse, organize, edit, move, copy, delete, and manage media files using the App's gallery functionality.

Processing: Media files are accessed and processed locally on your device. One2 Labs does not upload your photos or videos to its servers.

1.2. Albums, Folders, and Media Metadata

Data Accessed: Album and folder information and media metadata such as file names, file paths, file sizes, dates, timestamps, dimensions, and other metadata available through the Android media system.

Purpose: To organize, search, sort, display, and manage your media library.

Processing: This information is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to One2 Labs servers.

1.3. Media Access Permissions

Neu Gallery may request Android media or storage permissions required to provide gallery functionality.

Depending on your Android version and the features you use, these permissions may allow the App to:

  • Display photos and videos.
  • Create, edit, move, copy, or delete media files.
  • Organize albums and folders.
  • Access selected media that you choose to open or manage.
  • Share media when you explicitly initiate a sharing action.

These permissions are used only to provide the corresponding gallery functionality.

1.4. Clipboard Features

Neu Gallery may provide functionality that allows you to copy file names, paths, text, or other information to your device's clipboard.

Information copied to the clipboard is handled by the Android system clipboard and may be accessible to the operating system or other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

Neu Gallery does not control how other applications access or process clipboard information.

1.5. Internet Access

Neu Gallery may require internet access for certain functionality, including:

  • Processing in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.
  • Accessing optional online resources or support-related functionality, where applicable.
  • Other functionality that may be introduced in future versions of the App.

One2 Labs does not use the internet connection to upload your private photos, videos, or personal media library to its own servers.

2. Data Storage and Security

Local Processing

Personal media information used by Neu Gallery, including photos, videos, albums, folders, and media metadata, is primarily processed locally on your device to provide the App's functionality.

No Cloud Storage of Media Data

One2 Labs does not operate servers that store your private photos, videos, or personal media library.

Device Security

The security of information stored on your device depends in part on the security protections provided by your Android device and operating system.

We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated and using appropriate device security measures such as a screen lock.

No method of electronic storage or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

If the App offers paid features or in-app purchases, Neu Gallery may use Google Play Billing to process purchases.

Payment transactions are handled by Google Play. One2 Labs does not receive or store your complete payment card, bank account, or other full payment credentials.

Google may process purchase-related information, such as transaction information and purchase status, in accordance with Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Neu Gallery currently does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services to collect information about your media library or gallery activity.

4. Data Sharing

One2 Labs does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your photos, videos, albums, folders, or other private media information with third parties for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

When you explicitly choose to share a photo, video, or other media using Android's sharing functionality, the selected information may be provided to the application or service you choose. Such processing is controlled by that application or service.

Information may also be processed by Android, Google Play, or other platform services when required for the operation of your device, the App, or services you choose to use. Such processing is governed by the respective provider's policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Neu Gallery does not maintain copies of your photos, videos, or personal media library on One2 Labs servers.

Because your media files are stored on your device, you can manage or delete them using Neu Gallery, Android, or other applications that have access to your device's media storage.

Deleting media through Neu Gallery may permanently remove the selected files from your device, depending on Android system behavior and the action you choose. We recommend confirming deletion actions carefully.

Information stored by Android or other applications may not be deleted when Neu Gallery is uninstalled.

If you contact One2 Labs by email, we may retain the information you provide for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request or comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

Neu Gallery is not specifically directed toward children.

One2 Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to One2 Labs, please contact us at one2.labs@gmail.com .
