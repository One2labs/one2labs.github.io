2. How Smart AppLock Works

Smart AppLock – App Lock is designed to help users protect selected applications on their Android device from unauthorized access.

The App may allow you to protect applications using methods such as:

  • PIN or password protection
  • Pattern protection
  • Fingerprint or other supported biometric authentication
  • Automatic display of a lock screen when a protected application is opened

The App does not require you to create an account.

3. Accessibility Service

Smart AppLock uses the Android Accessibility Service API to provide its core app-locking functionality.

The Accessibility Service is used to detect when an application protected by the user is being opened so that Smart AppLock can display its lock screen before access is granted.

Accessibility information is used for the app-locking functionality and is not used to read, store, or transmit the contents of your protected applications.

We do not use the Accessibility Service to capture passwords, messages, photographs, videos, banking information, or other content from the applications you protect.

Important: Smart AppLock does not use the Accessibility Service to collect or upload the contents of your other applications. The service is used to determine when a protected application is opened and to present the AppLock screen.

4. Accessibility Service Disclosure and Consent

Because the Accessibility Service provides functionality that may not otherwise be obvious to users, Smart AppLock provides an in-app explanation of why the service is required before enabling it.

Users can choose whether to enable the Accessibility Service. If the service is disabled, the core application-locking functionality may not operate.

The Accessibility Service is not used for advertising, analytics, or sharing the contents of other applications.

5. Device and App Information

To provide the App's functionality and maintain reliability, Smart AppLock may process certain device and application information.

Depending on the features and services enabled, this may include:

  • Android operating system information

  • App version and configuration information
  • Application usage and interaction information
  • Diagnostic and crash-related information

The App does not require registration or a user account.

6. PIN, Pattern, and Security Information

Smart AppLock allows users to configure authentication information such as a PIN, password, or pattern to protect selected applications.

Authentication information is used to provide the AppLock functionality and is stored locally on the user's device using the App's local storage mechanisms.

We do not transmit your AppLock PIN, password, or pattern to our servers.

Smart AppLock does not have access to or collect the actual biometric data stored by Android. When biometric authentication is used, authentication is performed through Android's supported biometric APIs.

7. Overlay / Display Over Other Apps Permission

Smart AppLock may request permission to display content over other applications.

This permission is used to display the AppLock authentication screen when a protected application is opened.

The overlay permission is not used to read or collect the content displayed inside your other applications.
