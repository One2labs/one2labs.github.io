Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Neu Video Player ("Neu Video Player", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application.

Neu Video Player is a media player for local and network/streaming playback. The application is based on mpv-android and uses mpv/libmpv technology for media playback.

1. Information We Handle

  • Media Files: Video, audio, subtitle, and playlist files you choose to open or play.
  • Network Data: Server addresses, URLs, connection information, credentials, and other information required for SMB, FTP, WebDAV, HTTP, and HTTPS playback.
  • AI Subtitle Data: When you use optional AI subtitle features, selected subtitle-related content may be sent to third-party providers such as Google Gemini or Groq.
  • Crash and Diagnostic Data: Technical and diagnostic information may be processed to identify crashes, bugs, and compatibility issues.
  • Advertising Data: The free version may display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Purchases: Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing.

2. How We Use Information

  • Play and manage local and network media.
  • Provide subtitle and streaming features.
  • Provide optional AI-powered subtitle features.
  • Store application settings and playback preferences.
  • Improve performance, stability, and reliability.
  • Process purchases and subscriptions.
  • Display advertisements in the free version.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with or processed by third-party services when required to provide features you use, including Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, AI providers such as Google Gemini or Groq, subtitle or metadata services, crash/diagnostic services, and network servers selected by you.

4. Data Storage

Most application settings and playback-related information are stored locally on your device. Your local media files are not automatically uploaded to us simply because you open or play them.

You can remove locally stored application data through Android Settings or by uninstalling the application. Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies.

5. AI Subtitle Features

AI subtitle features are optional. When you use them, selected subtitle-related content or other information necessary to provide the requested feature may be sent to third-party AI providers such as Google Gemini or Groq.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. You should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information through AI features unless you are comfortable with it being processed by the applicable provider.

6. Advertising

The free version may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Advertising providers may process device, advertising, diagnostic, and other permitted information to provide and measure advertisements.

Premium versions may provide an ad-free experience where stated in the applicable purchase or subscription description.

7. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment processing and related payment information. We may receive limited purchase information necessary to verify and maintain your premium entitlement.

8. Your Choices

  • Choose which media files and network sources to access.
  • Choose whether to use optional AI subtitle features.
  • Manage application permissions through Android Settings.
  • Clear application data at any time.
  • Uninstall the application at any time.
  • Manage available advertising and privacy choices through Android or Google settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Neu Video Player is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children for profiling or similar purposes.

10. Third-Party Services

Neu Video Player may interact with third-party services including Google Play, Google AdMob, AI providers, subtitle or metadata services, crash/diagnostic services, and network servers selected by you. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.
