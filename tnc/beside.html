Privacy Policy

One2 Labs ("One2 Labs", "we", "us", or "our") develops Beside, an Android launcher application. This Privacy Policy explains how Beside accesses, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the App.

In short: Beside is designed to work primarily on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your personal information, installed-app list, launcher settings, favorites, folders, or widget configuration from Beside.

1. Information Beside Accesses

To provide launcher functionality, Beside may access information available through Android on your device, including information about applications installed on the device, application labels and icons, launcher activities, shortcuts, widgets, and other information required to display and launch applications.

Beside may also access information needed to determine device configuration, such as screen size or available window configuration, so that the launcher can adapt its layout and multitasking features.

This information is processed locally on your device to provide launcher features. Beside does not send this information to One2 Labs.

2. Personal Information

Beside does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, password, phone number, postal address, or other personal profile information in order to use the App.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from you for transmission to One2 Labs.

3. Installed Applications and App Information

As an Android launcher, Beside needs to access information about installed applications in order to display the Apps list, favorites, folders, app pairs, grouped applications, and launcher shortcuts.

This information can include application names, package identifiers, application icons, launchable activities, and shortcut information exposed by Android.

This information is used locally to provide launcher functionality. It is not uploaded to One2 Labs or sold to third parties by Beside.

4. App Pairs, Favorites, Folders, and Settings

Beside may store launcher configuration on your device, such as favorites, folders, hidden items, app-pair configuration, widget selections, dock configuration, appearance preferences, and other settings you choose.

These settings are used to provide and remember your launcher customization. They are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to One2 Labs.

5. Widgets and Shortcuts

Beside can display Android application widgets and shortcuts. Widget and shortcut functionality is provided through Android and the corresponding applications.

Beside does not control the data displayed inside a third-party widget. Third-party applications may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. You should review the privacy policy of the relevant application or widget provider if you have questions about its data practices.

6. Data Collection and Sharing

Beside does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

One2 Labs does not receive the installed-app inventory, launcher configuration, favorites, folders, or widget configuration stored and processed locally by Beside.

Beside does not intentionally transmit this information to external servers operated by One2 Labs.

7. Third-Party Applications

Beside can launch and interact with applications installed on your device. When you open a third-party application, that application may collect, use, or share information according to its own privacy policy.

Beside is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party applications, widgets, services, websites, or content that you access through the launcher.

8. Permissions

Beside may request Android permissions or system access where required for specific launcher functionality. The permissions available to the App depend on your Android version and device.

You can manage available permissions through Android system settings. Some features may not work if required permissions or system access are not granted.

9. Data Security

Because Beside is designed to process its launcher information locally, there is no One2 Labs server-side database containing your launcher configuration or installed-app list.

Information stored locally by the App is subject to the security of your Android device and its operating system. We recommend using the security features provided by your device, such as a screen lock and device encryption where available.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Beside does not retain your launcher information on One2 Labs servers. Information stored locally by Beside remains on your device until it is changed, removed by the App, cleared through Android settings, or removed when you uninstall the App, subject to Android's handling of application data and backups.

Because Beside does not require an online account, there is no Beside account containing personal data that you need to request us to delete.

11. Children's Privacy

Beside is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Beside.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

You should review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
