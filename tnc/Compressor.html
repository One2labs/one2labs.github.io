Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Thank you for using Video Compressor. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Video Compressor processes all videos locally on your device.
We do not collect, upload, or share your videos or personal information.

Information We Collect

Video Compressor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • Payment information

Video Processing

All video compression takes place entirely on your Android device.

Your videos never leave your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Permissions

Video Compressor requests only the permissions required to perform video compression and save compressed videos.

These permissions may include:

  • Photos and Videos access
  • Media access
  • Storage access on older Android versions

These permissions are used solely to allow you to choose videos and save the compressed output.

Internet Access

Video Compressor does not require an internet connection to compress videos.

If the application includes the INTERNET permission for Android compatibility or future functionality, it is not used to upload videos or collect personal information.

Advertising

Video Compressor does not display advertisements.

Analytics & Tracking

Video Compressor does not use any analytics or tracking services.

The app does not use:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Analytics
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Tracking SDKs
  • Third-party analytics services

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.

Since all processing happens locally, your videos remain entirely under your control.

Data Retention

The app does not retain copies of your videos or personal information.

Compressed videos are saved only to the location you choose on your device.

Children's Privacy

Video Compressor is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
