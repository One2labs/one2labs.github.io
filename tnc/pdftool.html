Privacy Policy for PDF Editor & Scanner

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

PDF Editor & Scanner is designed with privacy first. Most document processing happens entirely on your device. We do not upload your documents to our servers or use your files for advertising or analytics.

1. Introduction

PDF Editor & Scanner ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information when you use it.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store your personal documents or the contents of your PDF files.

The App does not intentionally collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Advertising ID
  • Usage analytics
  • User tracking information

Your documents remain under your control.

3. How Your Files Are Processed

All supported document operations are performed locally on your device whenever possible.

  • Edit PDF
  • Merge PDF
  • Split PDF
  • Compress PDF
  • OCR Text Recognition
  • Add Watermarks
  • Convert Images
  • Fill PDF Forms
  • Sign Documents
  • Scan Documents

Your files are never uploaded to our servers. Temporary files created during processing remain on your device and may be removed automatically after processing. Processed files are saved only to locations you choose.

4. Permissions

Storage Access

  • Open PDF and image files
  • Save edited documents
  • Files are accessed only when you explicitly select them

Camera (Optional)

  • Used only for document scanning
  • Images are processed locally on your device

5. Local Data Storage

The App stores limited information locally on your device, including:

  • Recent file history
  • Application preferences
  • Temporary cache
  • OCR language preferences (if applicable)

You can remove this information at any time by:

  • Clearing Recent Files
  • Clearing App Cache
  • Clearing App Data
  • Uninstalling the App

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Analytics SDKs
  • User tracking services
  • Cloud storage services
  • Social media integrations

The App uses open-source and Google-provided libraries for local functionality, including:

  • Apache PDFBox
  • Google ML Kit (On-Device OCR)
  • Jetpack Compose
  • AndroidX Libraries

These libraries are used only to provide application functionality and are not used by us to collect your personal documents.

7. Internet Usage

Most features work completely offline. If internet access is required for future optional features, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Data Security

  • Your documents remain on your device.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your PDF files.
  • The App uses Android's secure storage and file access mechanisms.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not store your documents or personal information on our servers:

  • No account is required.
  • No cloud data exists to delete.
  • Your files remain fully under your control.
  • You may delete your files, cache, preferences, or uninstall the App at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
