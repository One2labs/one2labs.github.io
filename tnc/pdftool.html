Privacy Policy for M PDFTools

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

M PDFTools is designed with privacy first. Most document processing happens entirely on your device. We do not upload your documents to our servers or use your files for advertising or analytics.

1. Introduction

M PDFTools ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use its features.

2. Information We Collect

M PDFTools does not collect or store your personal documents or the contents of your PDF files on our servers.

The App does not intentionally collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Advertising ID
  • Usage analytics
  • User tracking information

Your documents remain under your control and are processed locally on your device whenever possible.

3. How Your Files Are Processed

M PDFTools performs supported document operations locally on your device. Your documents are not uploaded to our servers for processing.

  • Edit PDF documents
  • Merge PDF files
  • Split PDF files
  • Compress PDF files
  • Reorder PDF pages
  • Rotate PDF pages
  • Extract PDF pages
  • Delete PDF pages
  • OCR text recognition
  • Add text and image watermarks
  • Convert images to PDF
  • Export PDF pages as images
  • Fill PDF forms
  • Add signatures
  • Scan documents
  • Password-protect PDF files

Your files are not uploaded to our servers. Temporary files created during processing remain on your device and may be removed automatically after processing. Processed files are saved only to locations selected by you.

4. Permissions

Storage and File Access

M PDFTools may request access to files when required to provide document-related functionality.

  • Open PDF and image files
  • Save edited documents
  • Export converted documents
  • Access files that you explicitly select

M PDFTools does not access your files unnecessarily.

Camera (Optional)

Camera access is requested only when you use document scanning functionality.

  • Used for scanning physical documents
  • Captured images are processed on your device
  • Camera access is not used for tracking

5. Local Data Storage

M PDFTools may store limited information locally on your device to provide application functionality, including:

  • Recent file history
  • Application preferences
  • Temporary cache files
  • OCR language preferences, if applicable

This information is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

You can remove locally stored application information by:

  • Clearing Recent Files
  • Clearing App Cache
  • Clearing App Data
  • Uninstalling the App

6. Third-Party Services and Libraries

M PDFTools is designed to provide its core document-processing features without requiring cloud processing or user accounts.

The App may use open-source and platform-provided libraries, including:

  • Apache PDFBox
  • Google ML Kit for on-device OCR
  • Jetpack Compose
  • AndroidX Libraries

These libraries are used to provide application functionality. M PDFTools does not use your documents for advertising, profiling, or tracking.

7. Internet Usage

M PDFTools is designed to work offline for its core PDF and document-processing features.

The App does not require cloud storage or uploading your documents to process supported files.

If future features require network access, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how that access is used.

8. Data Security

  • Your documents remain on your device.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your PDF files for processing.
  • No cloud account is required to use the core features of the App.
  • The App uses Android's secure storage and file-access mechanisms.

9. Children's Privacy

M PDFTools is designed as a general-purpose document utility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Since the App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information for its core functionality, users can use the App without submitting personal details to us.

10. Your Rights and Control

Because M PDFTools does not store your documents or personal information on our servers:

  • No account is required for core features.
  • Your documents remain under your control.
  • No cloud copy of your documents is maintained by us.
  • You may delete your files at any time.
  • You may clear application cache and local data.
  • You may uninstall the App at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
