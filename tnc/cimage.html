Privacy Policy

Neu Image Compress – MB to KB

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Thank you for using Neu Image Compress – MB to KB. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Your images stay on your device.
Neu Image Compress processes images locally. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your images or personal information.

Information We Collect

Neu Image Compress does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Location
  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • Payment information
  • Personal photos or images

Image Processing

All image compression and processing takes place entirely on your Android device.

Your images are not uploaded to our servers and are not transmitted to us for processing.

Images selected for compression remain under your control on your device.

Permissions

Neu Image Compress requests only the access necessary to allow you to select images and save compressed results.

Depending on your Android version, the app may use:

  • Photos and Videos access
  • Media access
  • Storage access on older Android versions

These permissions are used solely to allow you to select images for compression and save the resulting images.

The app does not use these permissions to collect or transmit your personal information.

Internet Access

Neu Image Compress does not require an internet connection to perform image compression.

If the application includes the INTERNET permission for Android compatibility or future functionality, it is not used to upload your images or collect personal information.

Advertising

Neu Image Compress does not display advertisements.

The app does not use advertising networks or advertising SDKs.

Analytics & Tracking

Neu Image Compress does not use analytics or tracking services.

The app does not use:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Analytics
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Tracking SDKs
  • Third-party analytics services
  • Behavioral tracking

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.

Since image processing takes place locally on your device, your images are not sent to us or third parties for processing.

Data Retention

Neu Image Compress does not retain copies of your images or personal information on external servers.

Compressed images are saved only to the location selected by you on your device.

You remain responsible for managing and deleting files stored on your device.

Children's Privacy

Neu Image Compress does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no personal information maintained by us for children.

Third-Party Services

Neu Image Compress does not use third-party services for advertising, analytics, tracking, or remote image processing.

The app's core image compression functionality operates locally on your device.

Security

Because your images are processed locally and are not uploaded to our servers, we do not maintain copies of your images on our systems.

You should still use normal device security practices, such as keeping your Android device updated and protected.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
