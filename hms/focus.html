Introduction

PDF Editor & Scanner respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use the application.

Information Collection

PDF Editor & Scanner does not require registration or login.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or other information that directly identifies you.

The application does not sell or rent your personal information.

Document Access and Local Processing

The application may request access to PDF files and images on your device only when necessary to provide features such as opening, editing, converting, scanning, and saving documents that you choose.

Your documents are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to One2Labs servers.

We do not use your documents for advertising, profiling, or other unrelated purposes.

Camera Permission

Camera permission is used only when you choose to scan documents using your device camera.

Images captured for document scanning are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to One2Labs servers.

Notification Permission

Notification permission, where available and granted by you, may be used to display document processing progress or other relevant application notifications.

Storage and Files

Files created, edited, converted, or saved using the application are stored on your device according to your actions and device settings.

You remain responsible for managing and deleting files stored on your device.

Third-Party Services

The application does not intentionally share your personal information or documents with third parties.

If the application uses third-party platform services required for distribution or device functionality, those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples may include services provided by Huawei Mobile Services (HMS) or Huawei AppGallery when applicable to the distribution or operation of the application.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the application.

Because documents and related processing are performed locally on your device, the security of those files also depends on the security of your device, operating system, and user account.

Data Retention

PDF files, images, and other documents created or processed by the application remain on your device until you delete them or they are removed through your device's storage management.

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your documents.

Children's Privacy

PDF Editor & Scanner is not specifically directed toward children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

The latest version will always be available on this page. The effective date shown at the top of this policy indicates when the current version became effective.
