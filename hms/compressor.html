Privacy Policy

Video Compressor is developed by Ankush Mishra. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information Collection

Video Compressor does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

All video compression is performed entirely on your device. Your videos are never uploaded to our servers.

Permissions

  • Photos & Videos / Storage – Used to select videos and save compressed videos.
  • Notifications (Optional) – Used to notify you when compression has finished.

These permissions are used only to provide the app's functionality.

Third-Party Services

Video Compressor does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or cloud processing services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information to any third party.

Children's Privacy

Video Compressor is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

隐私政策

Video Compressor 由 Ankush Mishra 开发。 我们尊重您的隐私，并致力于保护您的个人信息。

信息收集

Video Compressor 不会收集、存储或传输您的任何个人信息。

所有视频压缩均在您的设备本地完成， 您的视频不会上传到任何服务器。

权限说明

  • 照片与视频 / 存储权限：用于选择视频和保存压缩后的视频。
  • 通知权限（可选）：用于在视频压缩完成后发送通知。

这些权限仅用于实现应用的核心功能。

第三方服务

Video Compressor 不使用任何广告、数据分析、用户跟踪或云处理服务。

数据共享

我们不会出售、出租、共享或向任何第三方披露您的个人信息。

儿童隐私

Video Compressor 面向所有用户，不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。

隐私政策更新
