1. Introduction

Thank you for using Neu Video Player. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application uses, why certain permissions are required, and how your privacy is protected.

2. Personal Information

Neu Video Player does not require registration, user accounts, or sign in.

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell or share your personal information including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Payment information
  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • Biometric information

3. Local Media Access

The application requests permission to access videos stored on your device only to allow you to browse and play your own media files.

All local media processing occurs entirely on your device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers.

4. Internet Permission

Neu Video Player includes Internet permission to allow playback of online videos and network media that you choose to open.

Internet access is used only to retrieve the media content requested by you. We do not collect personal information while you use these features.

5. Third-Party Services

Neu Video Player does not use:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Advertising SDKs
  • User tracking services

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information to any third party.

7. Children's Privacy

Neu Video Player is not specifically intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not maintain databases containing user personal data.

Media files remain on your own device unless you choose to access online content directly.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Any updates will be published with the latest version of the application or on the official privacy policy page.
