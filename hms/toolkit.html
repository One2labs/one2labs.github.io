Introduction

PDF Editor & Scanner respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles your information.

Information Collection

PDF Editor & Scanner does not require registration or login.

The application does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information.

Document Access

The application requests permission to access PDF files and images stored on your device only to open, edit, convert, scan, and save documents that you choose. Your documents remain on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

Camera Permission

Camera permission is used only for scanning documents. All processing is performed locally on your device.

Notification Permission

Notification permission (if available) is used only to display document processing progress.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to any third party.

Children's Privacy

PDF Editor & Scanner is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
