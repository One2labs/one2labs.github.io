Privacy Commitment

One2 Phone is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect, upload, transmit, sell, rent, or share your personal information. All processing takes place entirely on your device.

Permissions Used

To provide phone and dialer functionality, the application may request:

  • Phone – to make and receive calls.
  • Contacts – to display and manage contacts.
  • Call Logs – to display recent call history.
  • Microphone – for voice calls.
  • Notifications – for incoming calls and important notifications.
  • Default Dialer permission – to function as your phone application.

These permissions are used only for the requested functionality and are never used to collect personal information.

Local Data Processing

All contacts, phone numbers, call logs, favorites, blocked numbers, caller information, and application settings are processed and stored locally on your device.

No personal information is transmitted to our servers because One2 Phone does not operate any server that stores user data.

Internet Access

If the application includes the INTERNET permission, it is used only for optional features that you explicitly choose to use, such as opening web pages or accessing online resources.

Your contacts, phone numbers, call history, SMS messages, recordings, and other personal information are never uploaded or transmitted over the Internet.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, disclose, or transfer your personal information to any third party.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available with the application or on the official privacy policy page.

隐私承诺

One2 Phone 致力于保护您的隐私。 我们不会收集、上传、传输、出售、出租或共享您的任何个人信息。 所有数据处理均在您的设备本地完成。

权限说明

为了提供完整的电话和拨号功能，应用可能申请以下权限：

  • 电话权限：用于拨打和接听电话。
  • 联系人权限：用于显示和管理联系人。
  • 通话记录权限：用于显示最近通话记录。
  • 麦克风权限：用于语音通话。
  • 通知权限：用于来电及重要通知。
  • 默认电话应用权限：用于作为系统电话应用运行。

这些权限仅用于实现应用功能，不会用于收集您的个人信息。

本地数据处理

联系人、电话号码、通话记录、收藏夹、黑名单、来电信息及应用设置均在您的设备本地处理和存储。

One2 Phone 不运营任何存储用户数据的服务器，因此不会向我们的服务器上传或传输任何个人信息。

网络权限

如果应用包含 Internet 权限，仅用于您主动使用的在线功能，例如打开网页或访问在线资源。

您的联系人、电话号码、通话记录、短信、录音及其他个人信息绝不会通过网络上传或传输。

信息共享

我们不会向任何第三方出售、出租、披露或共享您的个人信息。

隐私政策更新

本隐私政策可能会不定期更新，最新版本将始终提供给用户。
